# Development

To develop Swift Bitcoin you need a Swift toolchain installed on your system. Follow the instructions at https://www.swift.org/install/ get set up. If you are on a Mac with Xcode you already have you need.

To get started simply `swift build` from the terminal. Alternatively you can open the package with Xcode and hit `Command-B`.
